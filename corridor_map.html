I-25 / Santa Fe Dr · INRIX, StreetLight, Replica

About this map

INRIX segments are drawn start → end in the direction of travel and offset to the right, so the two directions sit side by side like carriageways. The slider picks the 5-minute bin; click a segment for its 24-hour profile.

StreetLight circles are the turning-movement intersections. Click one for the 12 movements at the slider's 15-minute bin (and the hour starting there), for the chosen day type.

Replica circles sit at census-tract centroids, sized by the number of trips in the export that start (origins) or end (destinations) in that tract. The export already filters to trips using the selected I-25 / Santa Fe links in the 5–6 PM window. The filter dropdown restricts to a mode or purpose.

Basemap tiles and the MapLibre library load from the internet; the data is embedded in this file.
